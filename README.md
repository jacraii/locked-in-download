# Locked In

**Less feed. More done.** Locked In is a free focus app for students. Tell the coach what you're avoiding, turn it into one task, then lock in: a timer runs and the apps and websites you choose stay on hold until it ends.

**[Download for Windows](https://github.com/jacraii/locked-in-download/releases/latest)** · Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, about 1 GB · [Website](https://locked-in-focus.vercel.app/)

## Install

1. Download `Locked-In-Setup-<version>.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/jacraii/locked-in-download/releases/latest).
2. Open it. It installs for your Windows account only (no administrator rights), sets up the coach and opens Locked In.
3. If Windows shows a warning, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Locked In isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it.

Uninstall any time from Windows Settings > Apps.

## What's inside

- **Coach:** works straight away with a private AI that runs on your computer, with no account or internet needed. It switches to a smarter online AI through OmniRoute when you connect a provider there.
- **Blocks:** YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, X, Netflix, Twitch or any site you add, in Chrome, Edge and most other browsers, plus Discord, Steam, Roblox or any app on your PC. Emergency unlock: Ctrl + Shift + F12.
- **Memory:** the coach remembers notes you give it and learns from your past sessions. Everything stays on your computer.

This repository only hosts the installer. Mac and Linux versions are on the way.
